# CiiiB — Invariants and Theory

## Working identity

**CiiiB** is a core build for browsing voiced reality.
It is not only a product, but a general engine for:
- Civilization Browser
- Market Browser
- Avatar
- Generated Cinema

These are not separate ontologies.
They are branded or configured surfaces of the same core.

The core is built on:
- `o` for ontology, manifestation, persistence, and addressable being
- `ww` for agentic reading, synthesis, continuation, and voiced unfolding

---

## First principle

Reality is not consumed only as facts.
Reality is also consumed as **voices**.

A civilization, a market, a demographic, an avatar, or a scene may each be treated as a voiced being whose state can be:
- read
- aggregated
- continued
- contrasted
- branched
- embodied

So the central object of CiiiB is not a document, record, or feed item.
It is a **voiced unfolding thing**.

---

## Core invariant: one engine, many branded faces

CiiiB, Market Browser, Avatar, and Generated Cinema must all share one core build.
They must not split into separate metaphysics.

The same inner engine must be able to operate at different scales:
- collective mind
- economic mind
- individual mind
- multi-character unfolding world

This means that scale may change, but ontology must remain stable.

---

## Ontological invariant

Every subject in the system is a thing that may have:
- identity
- inner structure
- multiple manifestations
- generation lineage
- agentic continuations
- projections into tensor-space

This means we do not model the world as flat tables or isolated objects.
We model it as addressable beings with unfoldings.

---

## `o` invariant

`o` is the substrate of manifested ontology.
It provides:
- persistent being
- typed trees
- manifestation terraces under one `__o_module__`
- coordinate suffixes for multiplicity and generation
- extraction of subtree or property views
- projection toward tensor-space

A thing in `o` is not only a static object.
It may be:
- a canonical visible manifestation
- a field of further manifestations
- a tensor of trees
- a lineage of generated versions

This makes `o` suitable not just for storage, but for ontological computation.

---

## `ww` invariant

`ww` is the substrate of voiced becoming.
It provides agentic operations such as:
- reading
- summarizing
- contrasting
- continuing
- critiquing
- branching
- role-conditioned response

`ww` does not float on top of random blobs.
It acts upon `o` things.
So agentic work always happens on addressable manifested beings.

---

## Voice invariant

A voice is not merely text.
A voice is an active civilizational or agentic projection of a being.

Examples:
- the voice of Ukraine today
- the voice of a market niche
- the voice of skincare advertising
- the voice of an avatar
- the voice of a scene or character in generated cinema

A voice may be produced from many sources, but once produced it becomes its own object of browsing, comparison, and continuation.

---

## Source-layer invariant

Civilizational reality has layers.
At minimum, the system should treat these as separable and comparable:
- news
- advertising
- private/social human voices
- demographic slices
- national/cultural slices
- historical slices
- generated/agentic interpretive layers

Truth is not only what humans say.
Truth is also what media, incentives, and advertising repeatedly tell people.
So different source layers must remain distinguishable.

---

## Browser invariant

A browser is not just a search interface.
A browser is a navigable field of voiced entities and their transformations.

So browsing may include:
- selecting a subject
- selecting a source layer
- selecting a demographic or national slice
- selecting time
- selecting a branch or generation version
- reading a continuation or dialogue with the selected voice

This means browsing is traversal of a manifested ontology, not just retrieval of documents.

---

## Tensor invariant

Any sufficiently stable subject may be projected as a tensor of trees.

This means:
- a subject may have many manifestations
- each manifestation may be internally structured
- the set of manifestations may be addressed by coordinates
- structural paths may be extracted across coordinates

This allows one to treat:
- a civilization
- a market
- an avatar
- a sequence of scenes

as coordinateable fields rather than isolated points.

---

## Generation invariant

Manifestations may differ by generation.
A later manifestation is not merely a copy.
It is a further unfolding of the same being.

This gives natural support for:
- state evolution
- hypothesis branching
- alternative interpretations
- critique/revision chains
- scene and story variation

Generation lineage must therefore be first-class, not hidden.

---

## Agentic invariant

An agent is not only a worker.
An agent is a being that produces further manifested states.

Therefore agentic operations should not merely mutate hidden state.
They should create browsable continuations.

This is especially important for:
- Civilization Browser, where a civilization voice may be continued or contrasted
- Market Browser, where niches and desires may be unfolded into strategies
- Avatar, where a persona may be deepened, questioned, or embodied
- Generated Cinema, where characters and worlds unfold scene by scene

---

## Comparative invariant

The system must support comparison between voices, not only generation of single voices.

Examples:
- Ukraine news voice vs Ukraine ad voice
- luxury skincare market voice vs budget skincare market voice
- demographic A vs demographic B
- one avatar branch vs another
- one scene continuation vs another

The browser is therefore comparative by nature.

---

## Dialogue invariant

Voices should not only be summarized.
They should be speakable with.

This means a user may enter into dialogue with:
- a civilization slice
- a market slice
- an avatar
- a branch of a character or scene

But such dialogue must remain grounded in the selected manifested subject and source layers.

---

## Brand invariant

Brand names are not separate engines.
They are views onto the same core.

Examples:
- **Civilization Browser** — browsing collective voiced reality
- **Market Browser** — browsing desire, value tension, and commercial persuasion
- **Avatar** — browsing and instantiating individual voiced beings
- **Generated Cinema** — browsing and unfolding voiced worlds in time through scenes

This family relationship must remain explicit.

---

## Product-theory invariant

The practical and the philosophical must remain one body.

This core must be able to serve both:
- as a commercially useful instrument
- as a deeper theory of voiced, manifested reality

So the theory must not be decorative.
It must directly justify product features and data structures.

---

## Theory formulae worth keeping

- **voiced reality is browsable**
- **a civilization is not only events, but a field of voices**
- **a market is a civilizational slice under tension of desire and exchange**
- **an avatar is an individually embodied voiced being**
- **generated cinema is voiced world-unfolding in scenes across time**
- **`o` gives addressable being**
- **`ww` gives voiced becoming**
- **one core, many branded surfaces**
- **tensor of trees**
- **generation lineage is first-class**
- **the browser traverses ontology, not merely documents**

---

## Short unifying statement

CiiiB is a core engine for browsing, comparing, and continuing voiced manifested reality across scales — from civilization, to market, to avatar, to cinema — using one shared ontology in `o` and one shared agentic becoming layer in `ww`.
